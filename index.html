Building a Career in Clinical Data Management

with Dr. Susan Ghazi

Susan's path went from cardiovascular research to a PhD in kidney physiology to Lead Clinical Data Manager — and almost none of it was planned. She talks about trusting what felt right at each turn, how her PhD skills show up in industry work every day, and what roles like clinical data management, CRA, and medical science liaison actually look like day to day.

About the show

A resource for the road, not just the finish line.

PhD: The Experience guides listeners through the multifaceted journey of doctoral study. Through her own experiences and conversations with fellow researchers and academics, Alexandra offers a comprehensive look at the challenges and triumphs of pursuing a PhD — and the practical advice that rarely makes it into a supervision meeting.

The goal is a supportive community where researchers can learn from one another and trade the tips nobody tells you at orientation.

About the host

A bit about me

I was born in Romania, raised in Greece, and moved to Germany at sixteen — I've always been drawn to connecting with people, across languages and across cultures. That instinct followed me straight into my PhD.

My first year was hard in ways I didn't expect, and it showed me how little support and honest information actually exists for PhD students. It's not just me — in Nature's global survey of over 6,000 PhD students, 36% reported seeking help for anxiety or depression caused by their doctoral studies. I started this podcast to close that gap: real conversations and practical advice, made easy to find, before people reach a breaking point.

Outside the lab and the mic, you'll usually find me near the sea, out in nature, with dogs, or spending time with my friends and family — the things that keep me steady through all of it.
